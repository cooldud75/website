# Assorted Bitcoin Graphs

### 1. Total Bitcoin Supply Curve
Tracks the total cumulative Bitcoin mined over time, showing the logarithmic approach toward the 21,000,000 hard supply cap across halving epochs.
![Total Bitcoin Supply Curve](/images/1-Number-Available.png)

***

### 2. Block Reward Step-Down Schedule
Visualizes the programmatic decay of the block subsidy, halving every 210,000 blocks starting from 50 BTC in 2009 down toward zero.
![Block Reward Halving Schedule](/images/2-Block-Reward.png)

***

### 3. Bitcoin Price on Exact Halving Dates
Highlights the spot price of Bitcoin on the exact calendar day of each halving event, tracing the long-term price expansion from $0.00 in 2009 to $63,803.00 in 2024.
![Bitcoin Price on Halving Dates](/images/3-Price-Per-Halving-Date.png)

***

### 4. Bitcoin Price Highs and Lows per Epoch
Compares cycle bottom lows against cycle peak highs recorded across each four-year halving epoch from Epoch 1 through Epoch 5.
![Bitcoin Price High and Low per Epoch](/images/4-Price-High-and-Low-per-Epoch.png)

***

### 5. Percentage Gain per Halving Cycle
Measures post-halving performance gains across cycles, illustrating the diminishing percentage return trend as Bitcoin's market capitalization grows (excluding 2012's initial off-the-charts expansion).
![Gain Percentage Per Halving](/images/5-Gain-Per-Halving.png)

***

### 6. Dollar Value of Block Rewards per Epoch
Tracks the nominal USD value of a single block reward at each epoch's price high versus its price low, showing how fiat price appreciation outweighs declining BTC issuance.
![Dollar Value of Block Rewards per Epoch](/images/6-Block-Reward-per-Epoch.png)

***

### 7. Side-by-Side Price and Reward Valuation
A grouped comparison evaluating Bitcoin's price floor and peak against the total fiat value of block subsidies generated across Epochs 1 through 5.
![Price and Block Reward Per Epoch](/images/7-Price-and-Block-Reward-per-Epoch.png)
